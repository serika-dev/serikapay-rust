# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a friendly place to contribute.

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree about code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks, in any project space.
- Keep discussion on topic and help newcomers find their way.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break these rules, and may
block people who repeatedly or seriously do. To report a problem, contact the maintainers
privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form or the
serika.dev organization profile.
